# Dovik visual identity

Design proposal for a local development process supervisor.

## Idea

Calm control of local processes. The compact D-shaped enclosure groups three horizontal process tracks into one recognizable mark. The lowercase wordmark keeps the identity approachable and practical.

Suggested supporting line: "Local processes. Under control."

## Palette

| Color | Value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Graphite | #171C22 | Primary text and dark backgrounds |
| Ivory | #F3F0E8 | Light backgrounds and reversed text |
| Orange | #FF6B3D | Symbol and restrained brand accents |

Use graphite for body text on ivory. Reserve orange for the mark, larger accents, and graphical tracks. Keep semantic process states separate from brand color and pair state colors with readable labels.

## Typography

Use a sturdy modern sans serif with open counters and restrained rounded corners for headings and prose. Use a monospace face for commands, process identifiers, and technical labels. The generated wordmark is custom-looking raster lettering, not a verified typeset font.

## Composition and usage

- Primary arrangement: orange symbol followed by graphite lowercase wordmark on ivory.
- Dark arrangement: orange symbol with ivory wordmark on graphite.
- Compact arrangement: symbol alone, centered with generous padding.
- Keep at least one track-height of clear space around the logo.
- Reuse three parallel tracks as a sparse supporting graphic, never as a busy background behind text.
- Use flat fills, consistent spacing, and simple alignment. Avoid gradients, shadows, distortion, and additional symbols inside the mark.

## Generation prompts

### Primary logo

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: primary horizontal logo for Dovik, a local development process supervisor used by developers.
Primary request: Create an original, beautifully resolved geometric symbol plus the exact lowercase wordmark "dovik". Design direction: calm control of several local processes, precision and practical confidence.
Symbol: a bold compact capital-D silhouette built from a strong vertical spine and a rounded right enclosure, with three short parallel horizontal process tracks carved as generous negative spaces inside. Make the three tracks part of one coherent D shape, not a separate server illustration. A distinctive, simple silhouette with generous negative space and optically balanced geometry. No small decorative details. Symbol should remain intelligible at small sizes.
Wordmark: exact text "dovik", d-o-v-i-k, lowercase, custom-looking sturdy modern grotesk sans serif, medium-bold weight, open counters, restrained slightly softened corners. Excellent kerning. The symbol is to the left, with deliberate clear space between symbol and lettering.
Color palette: solid burnt-bright orange #FF6B3D for symbol, solid graphite #171C22 for wordmark, uniform warm ivory #F3F0E8 background.
Composition: wide landscape canvas, a single centered horizontal logo occupying about 70 percent of canvas width, generous clean margin. Symbol about 1.3 times the lowercase wordmark height.
Style: professional flat vector-like graphic design, razor-sharp edges, restrained industrial editorial identity. Output is a raster logo image.
Constraints: one logo only, no brand board, no additional text or slogan, no mockups, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no texture, no glossy effects, no circuit boards, no cloud, no animals, no play triangle, no generic power button, no watermark.
```

### Identity presentation

The primary logo was supplied as the image reference.

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: compact visual identity presentation for Dovik.
Reference: the previous generated Dovik logo. Preserve its exact D-shaped orange symbol with three horizontal negative-space slots and its lowercase dovik wordmark. Reuse the same geometry, do not redesign.
Create an elegant landscape brand identity sheet with a precise editorial grid on a warm ivory #F3F0E8 opaque background. Upper half: large original logo, graphite wordmark and orange symbol. Small heading "DOVIK / VISUAL IDENTITY". Lower half: three restrained panels: (1) graphite #171C22 field with the symbol in orange and the same wordmark in ivory; (2) orange square with the symbol in graphite as an app-icon usage; (3) three color swatches labeled exactly "#171C22", "#F3F0E8", "#FF6B3D". At bottom a simple motif of three parallel horizontal tracks of varying length, derived from the symbol, plus the line "Local processes. Under control."
Professional flat graphic design, crisp edges, no perspective, no physical mockups, no gradients, no shadows, no texture, no decorative borders, no additional text. Consistent shapes across all applications, generous white space, beautifully balanced typography. This is a visual design proposal, not an application screenshot.
```
